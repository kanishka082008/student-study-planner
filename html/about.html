About StudyFlow
A simple study planner designed for students.

StudyFlow is a student-focused study planning platform designed to help students organize their daily tasks, manage study schedules, track academic progress, and stay consistent with their goals.

📋Organize

Keep your academic tasks organized in one place.

⏰Plan

Create a structured study schedule for your day.

📈Track

Monitor your progress and stay motivated.

Who is StudyFlow for?

StudyFlow is designed for students who want a simple way to organize their studies, assignments, practice sessions and academic goals.

  • Daily task management
  • Study schedule
  • Subject progress
  • Deadline tracking
  • Simple and clean interface
  • Responsive design
